🥇 Romanian Ordinal Numerals

Who is First, Second, or Third?

👑 The Exception: Number 1

Just like in English ("one" vs. "first"), the Romanian word for 1st is completely irregular. You don't use the word "unu". Instead, you learn a special word for it!

1st
primul
Masculine / Neuter

e.g., primul băiat (the first boy)

1st
prima
Feminine

e.g., prima fată (the first girl)

Note: There is also an older form întâiul / întâia, but primul / prima is much more common in everyday speech.

🥪 The "Sandwich" Formula (2 and up)

For all other numbers, we create ordinal numbers by placing the cardinal number (which you already know!) into a grammar sandwich. We put an article before it, and an ending after it.

Masculine & Neuter Nouns

al + [cardinal number] + lea

Example for 2: al doilea (the 2nd)

Feminine Nouns

a + [cardinal number] + a (or ea)

Example for 2: a doua (the 2nd) *Note we use "două" without the little hat on the 'ă'.
