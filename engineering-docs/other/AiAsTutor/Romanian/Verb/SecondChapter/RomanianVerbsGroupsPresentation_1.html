🏰 Kingdom I: The "-a" Verb Family

Master the regular verbs and the magic "-ez" words!

Welcome to Castle A! 🏰

Verbs in Kingdom I are the most common verbs in Romanian. Their infinitive name ends in the letter -a (after removing the helper word a).

From the First Chapter, we already know two important verbs in this family:

  • a pleca (to leave)
  • a mânca (to eat)

To help you expand your vocabulary, we invited 8 new verbs to join our Castle! Let's explore how they behave.

The Present Tense: Two Paths 🛣️

When changing Group 1 verbs in the present tense, they split down two different paths. Some use standard endings, while others use a special helper suffix -ez to sound smoother.

Path A: Regular Endings (No Suffix)

These verbs simply add standard endings directly to the verb stem. Let's look at a cânta (to sing):

Eu cânt I sing
Tu cânți You sing
El/Ea cântă He/She sings
Noi cântăm We sing
Voi cântați You all sing
Ei/Ele cântă They sing
⚠️ Watch out for sound changes! Some verbs change vowels in the singular and 3rd plural:
• a pleca: Eu plec ➔ El pleacă
• a juca: Eu joc ➔ El joacă
Path B: The Magic "-ez" Suffix (-ez)

Many Group 1 verbs add the helper suffix -ez for Eu/Tu/El/Ea and Ei/Ele. Let's look at a lucra (to work):

Eu lucrez I work
Tu lucrezi You work
El/Ea lucrează He/She works
Noi lucrăm (No suffix!) We work
Voi lucrați (No suffix!) You all work
Ei/Ele lucrează They work
💡 The Suffix-Free Zone: Notice that the Noi and Voi forms NEVER take the suffix! They are always clean and simple.

The Past Tense: Drop and Add! ⏪

To talk about the past in Romanian, we need the Participle form (the "done" word). For Kingdom I, this is incredibly simple!

👉 Formula: Drop the final -a and add -at!
a cânta
➔ drop -a ➔ cânt
➔ add -at ➔ cântat
a pleca
➔ drop -a ➔ plec
➔ add -at ➔ plecat
a lucra
➔ drop -a ➔ lucr
➔ add -at ➔ lucrat

Then, we combine it with our helper verbs (am, ai, a, am, ați, au) just like before:

• Eu am cântat = I have sung
• Tu ai jucat = You have played
• Noi am lucrat = We have worked
