🏰 The 4 Verb Kingdoms of Romanian

Sort, learn, and conquer the ending families!

Welcome to the Kingdom Map! 🗺️

In our first chapter, we met 16 powerful verbs (our action words) and learned how they change to talk about the Present, Past, and Future. But did you know that verbs are also sorted into four distinct families?

In Romanian grammar, these families are called Conjugations (Conjugări). They help us know exactly how a verb behaves when we change it! We classify them into 4 groups based on how their Infinitive name ends.

How to find a Verb's Family 🔍

Finding a verb's family is as easy as 1-2-3! Just follow these simple steps:

1
Find the Name

Start with the full name of the verb (the Infinitive). e.g., a pleca

2
Drop the "a" from the front of the infinitive verb

Remove the little helper word a from the front. e.g., pleca

3
Check the End!

Look at the very last letter(s). e.g., pleca ends in -a

💡 depending on whether the name ends in -a, -ea, -e, or -i/-î, the verb belongs to family 1, 2, 3, or 4!

The 4 Ending Castles 🏰

Here are the four kingdoms, filled with the 16 verbs we have already studied:

1st Kingdom: The "-a" Family -a
These are friendly, common verbs. Most new words in Romanian join this family!
a pleca (to leave)
a mânca (to eat)
2nd Kingdom: The "-ea" Family -ea
A small, exclusive club of helper verbs. They are super important!
a avea (to have)
a vrea (to want)
a putea (to be able to)
a vedea (to see)
a bea (to drink)
3rd Kingdom: The "-e" Family -e
These have short, punchy endings and are very fun to speak.
a merge (to go)
a face (to do)
a scrie (to write)
4th Kingdom: The "-i" Family -i / -î
Action-packed verbs. In the present tense, many love to add "-esc"!
a fi (to be)
a fugi (to run)
a vorbi (to speak)
a citi (to read)
a veni (to come)
a ști (to know)
