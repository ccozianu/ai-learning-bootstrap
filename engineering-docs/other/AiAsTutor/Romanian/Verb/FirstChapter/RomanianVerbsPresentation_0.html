🚀 Magic Words: Romanian Verbs!

Let's learn how to DO things!

Welcome, explorer! 🗺️

Today we are going to learn about Verbs. Verbs are action words! They tell us what someone is doing. Like jumping, reading, or playing.

Before we start, let's quickly remember our heroes (the Pronouns) who do the actions:

Eu = I   |   Tu = You   |   El/Ea = He/She
Noi = We   |   Voi = You all   |   Ei/Ele = They

The Name of the Verb 🏷️

Just like you have a name, verbs have a name too! This "name" is called the Infinitive.

In English, the infinitive usually has "to" in front of it (like to read or to do). In Romanian, it has the letter "a" in front of it!

a face
➔
to do
a citi
➔
to read

1. Present Tense: Happening NOW! ⚡

When we do something right now, the verb changes slightly depending on WHO is doing it.

Let's look at the verb a face (to do):

Eu fac
➔
I do
Tu faci
➔
You do
El/Ea face
➔
He/She does
Noi facem
➔
We do
Voi faceți
➔
You all do
Ei/Ele fac
➔
They do

2. Past Tense: Already Done! ⏪

To talk about the past in Romanian, it's just like saying "I have done" in English! We use a little helper verb (auxiliary) plus the "done" form of our main verb.

The helper verb is a form of "a avea" (to have), and the "done" form of a face is făcut (done).

Watch how the colors match perfectly!

Eu am făcut
➔
I have done
Tu ai făcut
➔
You have done
El/Ea a făcut
➔
He/She has done
Noi am făcut
➔
We have done
Voi ați făcut
➔
You all have done
Ei/Ele au făcut
➔
They have done

3. Future Tense: What Will Be! 🚀

When we talk about the future, we need another helper word. In English, we use "will". In Romanian, we use forms of "a voi" (will).

After the helper word, we just use the name of the verb (the Infinitive, but without the "a"). So for a face, we just use face.

Eu voi face
➔
I will do
Tu vei face
➔
You will do
El/Ea va face
➔
He/She will do
Noi vom face
➔
We will do
Voi veți face
➔
You all will do
Ei/Ele vor face
➔
They will do
💡 Bonus Tip: You can use this for ANY verb in the future!

To read (a citi): Eu voi citi (I will read)
To be (a fi): Ea va fi (She will be)
